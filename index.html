NUESTRA FORMA

No hacemos discursos. Hacemos juegos que se entienden jugando.

Cuando decimos que un juego tiene intención, hablamos de dirección, no de solemnidad. Cada prototipo parte de una pregunta sencilla: qué debería sentir la persona al jugar. Desde ahí elegimos reglas, ritmo, interfaz y límites. Si algo no ayuda a que la partida sea más clara, más tensa o más memorable, no entra.

La idea manda, pero la diversión tiene la última palabra.

Introducing GridMate · 25 Oct 2026

GridMate Chess: a fresh approach to digital chess through customization and community

Developed by Noke Games and currently in public beta, GridMate Chess offers a lively way to enjoy a classic strategy game. Its focus is social play and the identity players bring to their digital communities.

Read the feature

Gameplay and identity on the board

GridMate keeps the rules of chess while adding animations and visual feedback to moves, captures and check. Visual quality and reduced-motion options let players adapt this presentation to their preferences and device.

Players can equip themed boards, piece sets and effects, mixing individual actions or choosing a complete kit. Rival effects retain their identity during the game. Each viewer can choose how opposing pieces appear and whether to use their own board, the rival’s board or a split view. Cosmetics provide style, not a competitive advantage.

Play on the web and in Discord

GridMate is available in a web browser and as a Discord Activity. Friends can arrange private games by code or link. Public tables offer another way to meet opponents, with host approval in newly created tables.

A game can be shared, too

Once both players are seated, other people can watch if spectators are enabled for that table. Spectators cannot move pieces and can choose both players’ kits or an available shared player view. Communities can discuss the action through their usual Discord conversations without interrupting the game.

Created through independent development, GridMate aims to be an accessible and expressive space for casual and community chess, rather than a replacement for specialist professional analysis tools. The public beta continues to evolve.

GRIDMATE CHESS · BETA 0.7.1 · 25 Oct 2026

Your table. Your friends. Your style.

Beta 0.7.1 makes it easier to find a game and bring your own look to the board.

  • Public tables, ranked games and friend invites take center stage. A red ranked card signals rivals searching; choose the same time control.
  • Invite friends by code or link. In new public tables, the host accepts the next opponent before the game starts.
  • Mix movement, capture, check, mate, victory, defeat and nickname effects, or equip a complete set with your board and pieces.
  • Discover four new signature effects and Summer boards for your collection.
  • Watch enabled public games using both players’ kits or a player’s shared view. Rival effects keep their own identity.
  • Four free app themes, two free starter backgrounds for new and existing accounts, and ten more at 10 GridCoins each. Change them from Personalize, or choose no background.
  • Your public profile now shows your effects for each action.

GridMate Chess · BETA 0.6.1 · Oct 20, 2026

A new look. The same shared game.

Beta 0.6.1 brings guests into the same public and private tables, refreshes GridMate’s identity and brings the shop’s effect designs onto the board.

  • Join as a guest on the web or in Discord, create or join public and private tables, and invite friends by link—even if they have a registered account.
  • Guest sessions are temporary: no ranked play, purchases, rewards or saved progress. Create an account to keep your identity and collection.
  • Trails, captures, check and game-result effects follow the designs shown in the shop, with readable desktop/mobile sizing and respect for performance and reduced-motion settings.
  • Meet GridMate’s new logo, artwork and navigation icons.
